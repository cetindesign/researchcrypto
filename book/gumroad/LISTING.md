## 3) Description (Açıklama alanı — ana satış metni)

**New to crypto and afraid of getting scammed? You are exactly who this book is for.**

Crypto is exciting. It is also full of traps: fake support agents, phishing links, "giveaways," rug pulls, and coins that vanish overnight. Most beginners lose money not because crypto is too hard, but because no one ever taught them the safe way to start.

**Crypto 101: Real-World Knowledge for Beginners** is the calm, plain-English guide that fixes that. In about 40 pages you can finish in a weekend, you go from total beginner to making your first safe purchase and storing it securely, with the confidence to spot every common scam.

**What you'll learn**
- What crypto and blockchain actually are, in plain English (no jargon)
- The only vocabulary you truly need to get started
- How wallets really work, and how to protect your seed phrase
- How to choose an exchange and make your first purchase, step by step
- How to send and receive crypto without losing it to a simple mistake
- How to recognize and beat every common scam (the chapter that pays for the book)
- A complete security checklist to lock down your accounts
- How to invest calmly with dollar-cost averaging and avoid emotional mistakes
- Which advanced, high-risk things to avoid as a beginner, and why

**Why this book is different**
- Safety-first: built around not losing your money, not around hype.
- Actionable: every chapter ends with a checklist or a step-by-step you can follow.
- Short and clear: no filler, finishable in a weekend.
- Written for absolute beginners: every term is defined the first time you see it.

**What you get**
- A cleanly formatted PDF, ready to read on any device
- Copy-and-follow checklists for wallets, security, and safe transfers
- A glossary and a first-week action plan

Start smart. Start safe. Your future self will thank you.

_Educational content only. This book is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Crypto is volatile and risky; never invest more than you can afford to lose._
