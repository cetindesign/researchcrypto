# Book Outline — *Crypto Without the Scams* (working title)

**Angle:** Safety-first / anti-scam beginner guide
**Length target:** ~30-40 pages · **Price:** $9
**Promise:** Go from zero to your first *safe* crypto purchase — without getting scammed.

Page counts are approximate and used to keep the book lean and actionable.

---

## Front matter (~2 pp)
- Title page
- Disclaimer (educational only, not financial advice)
- Who this book is for / who it's *not* for
- How to use this book (weekend-ready, checklist-driven)

## 1. Introduction — Why beginners lose money (and how to not be one) (~2 pp)
The three ways newcomers get burned: scams, mistakes, and hype. This book kills all three.

## Part 1 — Understand the Basics (fast)
### 2. What crypto actually is, in plain English (~3 pp)
Money without a middleman; what a blockchain is (analogy-driven); what problem it solves — no jargon.
### 3. The only vocabulary you actually need (~3 pp)
Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, "coins vs tokens," wallet, exchange, key vs address, gas/fees. A tight, usable glossary-in-context.

## Part 2 — Get Started Safely
### 4. Wallets 101: where your crypto really lives (~4 pp)
Custodial vs non-custodial, hot vs cold, and the single most important object in crypto: your **seed phrase**. "Not your keys, not your coins."
### 5. Choosing an exchange & your first purchase — step by step (~4 pp)
CEX vs DEX, what to look for, KYC, and a literal click-by-click first buy. Start small.
### 6. Sending & receiving without losing your coins (~3 pp)
Addresses, networks (why sending on the wrong network loses funds), fees, confirmations, and the **test transaction** habit.

## Part 3 — Protect Yourself (the core of the book)
### 7. The scam playbook: how crooks target beginners (~5 pp)
Phishing, fake support, "giveaways," fake apps, rug pulls, and romance/"pig-butchering" scams. Universal red flags + golden rules. *(This is the sample chapter.)*
### 8. Your security checklist (~4 pp)
2FA done right, seed-phrase storage, cold wallets, revoking approvals, and the mistakes that drain wallets. A copy-and-follow checklist.

## Part 4 — Smart Beginner Habits
### 9. Investing vs trading, DCA & a calm long-term mindset (~3 pp)
Spot only for beginners; dollar-cost averaging; position sizing basics; not betting the rent.
### 10. Advanced & high-risk stuff — and why to wait (~2 pp)
Leverage, futures, DeFi, NFTs, memecoins: what they are in one line each, and an honest "not yet, here's why."

## Back matter (~3 pp)
- Glossary (quick reference)
- Your first-week action checklist
- Resources & final disclaimer

---

### Why this sells
- **Fear-solving angle:** the #1 beginner blocker is "I'll get scammed." This book's core promise addresses it head-on.
- **Actionable:** checklists and step-by-steps, not theory. Finishable in a weekend.
- **Upsell path:** a natural sequel on intermediate topics (trading, DeFi) later.
